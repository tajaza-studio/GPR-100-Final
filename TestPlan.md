# Script

Play through the game a couple of times, using a variety of skills and selecting different targets each time. Pay attention to how you feel using the UI and controls, and how the character and enemy health feels.

# Questions

## Question 1

How good did the controls feel? (Scale of 1-6 with option to elaborate)

## Question 2

How easy was the UI to understand? (Scale of 1-6 with option to elaborate)

## Question 3

Did the character health seem too high or low? (Scale of 1-6 with option to elaborate)

## Question 4

Did the enemy health seem too high or low? (Scale of 1-6 with option to elaborate)

## Question 5

How easy was it to understand the effects of character skills? (Scale of 1-6 with option to elaborate)
